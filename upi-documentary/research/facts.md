## Things the script deliberately does NOT claim
- That customers will be charged directly (the rule forbids it). The script only says costs *can* show up indirectly — framed as general economics, not as a fact.
- That any petrol pump actually stopped UPI after 16 Oct (the fee was deferred on 13 Oct, so we only report the announcement).
- Any figure for losses of a specific company (not verified).
- The "13 crore" or other political numbers from unrelated stories.
