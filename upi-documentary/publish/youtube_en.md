# TITLE
Who Really Pays for "FREE" UPI? | UPI Charges Above ₹2,000 Explained

# DESCRIPTION
UPI is getting a 0.4% MDR on merchant payments above ₹2,000. So is UPI no longer free? And who has been paying the ~₹20,000 crore a year it costs to run? A cinematic documentary built on government documents, NPCI data and Supreme Court records.

800 million UPI payments a day. Tea, petrol, rent, all in 2 seconds with zero fees. But the world's largest real-time payment system doesn't run for free. In this video: what the UPI MDR is, what the new UPI rules of 2026 actually say, how they may affect you, and whose name was on the bill all along.

✅ UPI New Rules 2026 at a glance
• P2P transfers (friends, family, self): completely FREE, any amount
• Merchant payments up to ₹2,000: FREE
• P2M above ₹2,000: 0.4% MDR, paid by the merchant, not the customer. Capped at ₹300 for ₹75,000+
• Railways, telecom, insurance, fuel: flat ₹5 per transaction
• Small merchants receiving up to ₹1 lakh/month: zero MDR
• Was due on 15 Oct 2026; on 13 Oct NPCI deferred it to "post Diwali", no new date yet
• Supreme Court refused to stay it on 28 Sep 2026
